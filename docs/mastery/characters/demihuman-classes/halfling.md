# Halfling

He knew that standing toe-to-toe with the ogre was a good way to get himself killed, yet here he was, dodging about, trying to avoid being turned into jam. However, the ogre was awfully slow. He slipped around to cut the tendon along the back of its left ankle. Boots would probably have protected, but the creature barely wore a loin cloth. Moving quickly, he brought the thing to is knees. Then its neck was in reach. Perhaps toe-to-toe is better than at a distance, since they are so clumsy.

He is a halfling. A demihuman who keeps to himself, mostly. The big folk are always too loud and almost always too angry to spend any time around. They don't know how to properly enjoy a good meal or a good drink without getting into fisticuffs. Naturally stealthy and agile, he makes as good a thief as he does a warrior, and oftentimes better than either of the human variety.

## Equipment Restrictions

## Halfling Experience Table

<span id="experience"></span>**Experience.** When this number of Experience Points have been earned, you automatically move up to the next Level of Experience.

<span id="title"></span>**Title**. You should use this title when talking with other characters. Instead of saying "I'm Touchberry, a Fifth Level Halfling," you should say "I'm Touchberry, the Homebody."

<span id="health"></span>**Health**. You gain 2 health per 3 levels of experience in addition to any health gained from your endurance.

<span id="skills"></span>**Skills**. At first level, you begin with 4 Skill Mastery points, which must be spent immediately on Skills. You may use these points to advance Skills to Untrained mastery, but no further at this level.

* As a Halfling, you can learn the following skills: *[Alertness](../../skills/alertness.md)*, *[Athletics](../../skills/athletics.md)*, *[Trade (Alchemist, Blacksmith, Healer)](../../skills/trade/index.md)*, *[Lore](../../skills/lore.md)*, *[Presence](../../skills/presence.md)*, *[Tactics](../../skills/tactics.md)*, *[Thievery](../../skills/thievery.md)*, and *[Wilderness](../../skills/wilderness/index.md)* skills.
* You learn *[Stealth](../../skills/stealth.md)* as part of the *Unobtrusive* class ability for free.
* You gain 1 Skill Mastery point per level. At level 6, this increases to 2 points per level. At level 16, this increases to 3 points per level. At level 26, this increases to 4 points per level.

<span id="spell-mastery"></span>**Spell Mastery**. This class does not have access to magic.

<span id="weapon-mastery"></span>**Weapon Mastery**. At first level, you begin with 3 Weapon Mastery points, which must be spent immediately on Weapon Types or Combat Styles. You may use these points to advance Weapon Types and Combat Styles to Untrained mastery, but no further at this level.

* You gain 1 Weapon Mastery point per level. At level 11, this increases to 2 points per level. At level 21, this increases to 3 points per level.
* Starting at level 2, each time you invest a point into a Weapon Type, you can invest one point for free into a single Combat Style, up to Master mastery.
* You can obtain Master mastery with a halfling.

## Leveled Abilities

You gain abilities below as you increase level.

### Mortal

As a Mortal being, you have no innate damage immunity or vulnerability. You are connected to the Void magic form. Only Void magic can Charm, Control, summon, or banish you, or restore your mind (see *[Magic Mastery](../../magic/index.md)*, [Connected Form](../../magic/exorcism-enchantment-shapeshifting.md#connected-form)). All other magic affects you normally.
