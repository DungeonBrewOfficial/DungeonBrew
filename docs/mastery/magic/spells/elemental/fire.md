<span id="cost_7"></span>**Cost:** (1 + 1 + 0) × 1 = 2.

You seize control of an existing fire within 30 feet and reshape it within its square: compress a bonfire into a candle flame that burns as hot as the bonfire, draw a hearth fire back from a doorway, or twist a torch's flame into a spike.
