# Asura

Asura are sustained by Sacred magic: life, purity and restoration. Angels, celestial guardians and holy beasts are all Asura. Mortal faith shapes them: what enough people believe is holy, and tell stories about as holy, becomes Sacred.

Asura want what Sacred magic is: things kept pure, preserved and restored. Mortals call that good; the Asura do not think in those terms. A Cherub will burn a starving thief at the threshold it guards as readily as a murderer, and the purity an Asura serves does not bend to mortal need. A party meets them guarding what they were set to guard, purging what they judge corrupted, and pursuing purposes that rarely ask what mortals want.

<span id="classification-package"></span>**Classification package (all Asura):** Immune to Sacred damage. Impotence on trials vs Profane damage. Connected to Sacred magic.

<span id="kingdoms"></span>**Kingdoms.** Asura belong to one of two kingdoms. **Amesha Spenta** are Asura numen, powerful enough to grant magic to those who devote themselves to them: numen that worship placed on the Sacred side of its faith. **Yazata** are all other Asura: beings that serve the Amesha Spenta or come from them, and creatures that holy stories have made Sacred.

## Agathodaimon (Tier 2 Leader)

*Division 4, Tier 2, Leader. ½ Asura Yazata (Sacred).*

*Good Spirit, Protective Genius*

A small protective spirit — a guardian of a person, family, or household rather than a place. Each agathodaimon is bound to a specific creature or lineage, remaining near its ward and warding away evil. They appear as luminous serpents, small winged figures, or simply as a warm presence. They are not powerful combatants — their strength is in protection and bolstering those they watch over.

## Cherub (Tier 3 Champion)

*Division 6, Tier 3, Champion. 1×1 Asura Yazata (Sacred).*

*Guardian, Threshold Keeper*

A Cherub is a guardian, the one that stands at the threshold. Cherubim are bound to guard a specific place, object, or being, and they do not leave until their purpose is fulfilled or their ward is destroyed. They appear as composite beings: part beast, part human, part something that doesn't map to mortal taxonomy. Eyes where eyes shouldn't be. Wings that fold in geometries that hurt to look at. Their presence radiates Sacred energy that burns the profane and unsettles the mortal.
