# Asura

Asura are beings sustained by Sacred magic — the force of life, purity, and restoration. They are not a primordial category of being. They are what numen become when mortal worship assigns them to the Sacred half of the moral framework. Angels, celestials, and divine guardians are numen reshaped by faith into embodiments of the Sacred.

All creatures in this file are **Asura Yazata (Sacred)** — lesser Sacred beings that serve or emanate from the Amesha Spenta (Asura numen).

**Builder notes:** Asura are the least populated combat-creature domain because most Sacred beings encountered by PCs would be allies, patrons, or narrative forces rather than enemies. The creatures below represent situations where an Asura opposes the party — a guardian they must bypass, a being whose purpose conflicts with theirs, or a corrupted angel. These are proof-of-concept stat blocks, not a comprehensive bestiary.

**Classification package (all Asura):**
- **Benefit:** Immune to Sacred damage. (Sustained by Sacred magic — Sacred energy is native to them.)
- **Cost:** Impotence on trials vs Profane damage. (Sacred nature is universally opposed to Profane.)
- **Connected to Sacred magic.** (Casters need Sacred Form to charm, bind, banish, or otherwise magically interact beyond dealing damage.)

These are free and inherited by every Asura creature, including Minions.

## Cherub (Tier 3 Champion)

*Division 6, Tier 3, Champion. 1×1 Asura Yazata (Sacred).*

*Guardian, Threshold Keeper*

A Cherub is not a chubby infant with wings. It is a guardian — the one that stands at the threshold. Cherubim are bound to guard a specific place, object, or being, and they do not leave until their purpose is fulfilled or their ward is destroyed. They appear as composite beings: part beast, part human, part something that doesn't map to mortal taxonomy. Eyes where eyes shouldn't be. Wings that fold in geometries that hurt to look at. Their presence radiates Sacred energy that burns the profane and unsettles the mortal.

## Agathodaimon (Tier 2 Leader)

*Division 4, Tier 2, Leader. ½ Asura Yazata (Sacred).*

*Good Spirit, Protective Genius*

A small protective spirit — a guardian of a person, family, or household rather than a place. Each agathodaimon is bound to a specific creature or lineage, remaining near its ward and warding away evil. They appear as luminous serpents, small winged figures, or simply as a warm presence. They are not powerful combatants — their strength is in protection and bolstering those they watch over.
