# Daeva

Daeva are sustained by Profane magic: dead matter, the threshold of death, disease and decay. Profane is the half of the cycle where life ends and returns to the earth, and the Daeva are the beings it sustains. The walking dead are the Daeva most often met, but far from the only ones.

Daeva want what Profane magic is: what was alive brought to stillness, what was whole unwound. Mortals call that evil; the Daeva do not think in those terms, any more than rot is cruel. A party meets them where death has been and not finished: battlefields, barrows, plague-houses, the halls of the long dead, wherever something was buried with a purpose it never let go.

<span id="classification-package"></span>**Classification package (all Daeva):** Immune to Profane damage. Impotence on trials vs Sacred damage. Connected to Profane magic.

<span id="kingdoms"></span>**Kingdoms.** Daeva belong to one of two kingdoms. **Div** are Daeva numen, powerful enough to grant magic to those who devote themselves to them. **Druj** are all other Daeva.

<span id="gestalts"></span>**Gestalts.** **Undead** are mortals whose natural life cycle has been overwritten by Profane magic. Whatever they were in life, what sustains them now makes them Daeva.

### Nasu (Tier 5 Legendary)

*Division 11, Tier 5, Legendary. Daeva Druj (Profane).*

The demon of carrion, the fly that settles on the dying. The moment a body loses its last breath she rushes into it, and that is what makes it a corpse. Everything that touches the dead she has entered sickens. She has no body of her own. Where death came all at once, in a plague-town or on a battlefield, she is in all the dead together, and the walking dead there are her hands.

<span id="what-the-stories-say"></span>**What the stories say.** Daeva Lore can turn up these stories. Which of them are true is the GM's choice.

- <span id="success"></span>**Success:** It is the Nasu, the demon of the dead. Where she is, the dead walk and the living sicken. Fire burns her, but the smoke of her burning dead is poison.
- <span id="success-by-2"></span>**Success by 2:** One story about what holds her, one about what drives her off, and one about what ends her, which the GM chooses and may make different for each PC.
    - *What holds her in a body:* a dog's gaze on the dead; only a four-eyed dog's gaze; the Grim of a burying ground; the shadow of a carrion bird passing over; a prayer against pollution spoken over the dead.
    - *What drives her off:* a dog walked along the path of the dead three times, or nine if it goes unwillingly; the bodies carried out by two, never one; a true purifier's rite over the field.
    - *What ends her:* brought to nothing while held in a body; every body in her field carried out by two and given to the birds; a true purifier's rite completed over the body that holds her. A false purifier only makes her stronger.
- <span id="success-by-4"></span>**Success by 4:** All of them, and the knowledge that the stories disagree.

<span id="for-the-gm"></span>**For the GM:** One story of each kind is true of this Nasu; settle them before play. Whatever holds her pins her to a pack (Many Bodies). Whatever drives her off counts as Driven Away. Whatever ends her is the only way to destroy her (Ended).
