# Athletics

## Structure

Athletics covers physical prowess, movement techniques, and bodily resilience.

Athletics pairs with different attributes depending on the task: Strength for lifting, pushing, and pulling; Agility for quick jumps, sprinting, and rapid movements; Physique for technical climbing, balance, and swimming strokes; Endurance for long-distance running, sustained swimming, and holding breath. DCs are set by the GM.

At each mastery level, you choose one Athletics discipline and one Athletics ability.

## Athletics Disciplines

<span id="balance"></span>**Balance:** Keeping your footing on narrow, uneven, or unstable surfaces, such as ledges, ice, rope bridges, or a ship's deck in a storm. The GM sets the DC by how narrow or unsteady the footing is, and raises it if you are fighting or carrying a load.

<span id="climbing"></span>**Climbing:** Ascending surfaces without established handholds, such as cliff faces, castle walls, or trees with sparse branches. A rope or ladder needs no trial. The GM sets the DC by how sheer the surface is, and raises it for rain, crumbling stone, or climbing under attack.

<span id="jumping"></span>**Jumping:** Clearing gaps, vaulting obstacles, and reaching elevated surfaces. The GM sets the DC based on distance and conditions. Covers horizontal leaps, vertical reach, and running vs. standing attempts.

<span id="lifting"></span>**Lifting:** Sustained exertion beyond normal carry capacity: holding open a portcullis, dragging a wounded ally, throwing heavy objects. Without this discipline, you are limited to what your carry capacity allows and cannot attempt feats of raw strength such as bending bars or forcing reinforced doors.

<span id="running"></span>**Running:** Sprint speed and sustained distance. Without this discipline, bipedal characters cap at x4 speed multiplier. With it, x5 and x6 sprint speeds are available. When you travel on foot, you have Dominance on your Endurance trials for Pushing the Pace.

<span id="swimming"></span>**Swimming:** Moving through water against currents, rough seas, or undertow, and fighting while submerged. Swimming also covers holding your breath for long stretches. Calm, shallow water needs no trial. The GM sets the DC by how rough the water is.

### PHYSIQUE ABILITIES

<span id="muscle-memory"></span>**Muscle Memory:** When you actively defend against an attack and take no durability or health damage, the next attack against you from the same source has Impotence.

<span id="poised"></span>**Poised:** Reprisal Attacks against you have Inferiority.

<span id="tumble"></span>**Tumble:** When you drop, reduce the DC of your Falling trial by your Physique tier. When you fall, you have Dominance on your Falling trial. On landing, you can tuck and roll into an adjacent space. Obstacles and difficult terrain caused by objects (rubble, furniture, low walls, barricades) do not impede your movement, and you can leap onto elevated surfaces up to your height as part of normal movement without a trial.

<span id="wall-run"></span>**Wall Run:** You can run on vertical surfaces (walls, cliff faces) or bounce between two vertical surfaces within 10 feet of each other. You can move up to 5 feet + (5 feet × Physique tier) per round on walls, whether moving horizontally or vertically. This is your maximum wall running distance per round regardless of your maximum movement speed or how many movement actions you use. You must end your movement on solid ground (platform, ledge, rooftop) or you fall. You cannot attack or defend while wall running. The surface must be relatively solid (stone, wood, brick — not ice, water, or actively crumbling).
