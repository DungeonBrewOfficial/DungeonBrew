# Tonics

## Coffee

*Roasted beans, brewed black and bitter. Night watchmen and scholars swear by it.*

Coffee is administered through ingestion. After one round, a creature that drinks it shakes off some of its exhaustion and grows sharp-eyed, but jittery. When it wears off, the exhaustion comes back worse, unless another cup puts it off.

|:---|:---|:---|:---:|
| Single strength | Reduce Exhausted by up to 1 tier for the duration. Dominance on Perception trials, including Startle | Impotence on Agility trials | 1 ss |

## Valerian

*A rank-smelling root, the herbalist's remedy for sleeplessness and nerves.*

Valerian is administered through ingestion. After one round, a creature that takes it grows calm and steady, and taken before resting, it sleeps better. It slows the body, though, and its smell carries: beasts and predators come looking.

| Strength | Effect | Side effect | Price |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|
| Single strength | +1 on Startle and Demoralized trials. Taken before resting, +1 health and +1 magan from that rest | Slow phase. Its smell draws beasts\* | 1 sp |

\* The smell raises the GM's encounter risk, as tallow candles do.
