# Anodynes

Anodynes dull pain and bring sleep.

## Aqua Vitae (*aqua ardens*, the "water of life")

*Wine distilled until it burns: the physician's cordial, the soldier's courage, and the strongest spirit known.*

Aqua vitae is administered through ingestion. After one round, a creature that drinks it feels less pain and, if it's distilled twice, holds its nerve, but it may also get drunk. Masked, it passes as a flavored cordial or hippocras. It's also a solvent, and a fuel that burns almost invisibly.

## Dwale (the sleeping drink)

*Boar's gall, hemlock, poppy, and henbane in wine: "a drink to make a man sleep whilst men carve him." The same word meant a deadly drug, and with good reason.*

Dwale is administered through ingestion, or inhaled from a Thickened soaked sponge, the *spongia somnifera*. After one round, a creature that takes it grows drowsy or falls asleep, deeply enough for a surgeon to work, though the stronger doses risk poisoning the sleeper. Forced on someone, it's a knockout drug.

## Mandrake (*mandragora*)

*The man-shaped root that "shrieks when pulled." In wine it brings sleep, dulls pain, and opens strange doors in the mind. Often faked with carved bryony.*

Mandrake is administered through ingestion, usually in wine, or by contact as a Thickened ointment. After one round, a creature that takes it thinks more sharply but moves more clumsily, and its mind may wander into confusion. Much of what's sold is carved bryony.

## Opium (poppy, *opium thebaicum*)

*Dried tears of the poppy, carried from distant fields. The only true painkiller of the age, and the hidden heart of every theriac. Those who take it nightly for sleep are the ones it keeps.*

Opium is administered through ingestion, or by contact as a Thickened plaster. After one round, a creature affected by opium feels less pain from its Injuries and Traumas, but its physical responses slow. Taken before resting, it brings a deep sleep, which is what makes it so addictive.
