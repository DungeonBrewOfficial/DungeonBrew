# Nostrums

Nostrums are the wonder-cures of the age: theriac, mumia, unicorn horn, potable gold. Most are fakes. A few really work for something small. And in this world, a rare few are **true**.

## Three Kinds

| Kind | What it does | Examples | How you get it |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Effective** | One small thing, though it's sold for a big one | Bezoar, snowdrop, viper wine | Bought, if you can find a genuine one |
| **Fake** | Nothing, whatever it's sold to do | See [Fake Nostrums](#fake-nostrums) | Sold everywhere |
| **Legendary** | What the legends promise | Ambergris, the blood draughts, hippomanes, moly, mumia, stag's heart bone, theriac | Learned by mastery level (see [Legendary Nostrums](#legendary-nostrums)), or granted by the GM |

## Legendary Nostrums

The common version of a legendary nostrum does nothing. The secret is one ingredient no apothecary has, and it comes from something huge and dangerous. The Nostrum category lets you recognize a true one, found as treasure, as real.

Legendary recipes come with the Nostrum category, one at each mastery level:

| Mastery | Legendary recipe |
|:---|:---|
| Untrained | Blood draughts (warm blood; hot blood at Skilled, the hottest at Master) |
| Basic | Mumia |
| Skilled | Theriac |
| Expert | Choose one: ambergris, hippomanes, moly, or stag's heart bone |
| Master | Choose one of those not yet chosen |
| Grand Master | Choose one of those not yet chosen |

If you take the Nostrum category at a later mastery level, you gain every legendary recipe up to your current mastery level at once. The mastery tree gives six of the seven; the GM can grant the last, or any other recipe, when it fits the campaign. The GM controls the ingredients.
