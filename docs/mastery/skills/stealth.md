# Stealth

## Structure

Stealth covers avoiding detection, ambush tactics, and operating unseen. Moving silently and remaining hidden relies on precise, controlled movement and muscle memory.

Stealth pairs with different attributes depending on the task: Physique for sneaking and hiding; Charisma for disguise and impersonation. DCs are set by the GM.

<span id="stealth-mastery-dc"></span>**Stealth Mastery DC:** Stealth mastery level + Physique tier - Worn Armor and Shield Encumbrance. When you are [Concealed](../core/conditions.md#concealed "Perception trial to detect. Concealed measures how well others know where a creature is. It comes from two kinds of source: Tiers: Obscured, Shrouded, Hidden.") through Sneaking or Vanish, Perception trials to search for you are against this DC.

## Mastery Progression

<span id="untrained-opportunist-sneaking-disguise"></span>**Untrained: Opportunist + Sneaking + Disguise**

<span id="sneaking"></span>**Sneaking:** You are able to conceal yourself or move quietly without attracting attention or being detected. You cannot use this skill while being observed, though it is possible to be concealed from one creature and not from another, at least until you are pointed out. You can conceal yourself effectively, providing there is cover or shadows to hide in, and move silently for the duration of your concealment.

During Exploration: When you begin sneaking, you start at Tier 3 [Concealed](../core/conditions.md#concealed "Perception trial to detect. Concealed measures how well others know where a creature is. It comes from two kinds of source: Tiers: Obscured, Shrouded, Hidden.") (Hidden) unless the GM determines otherwise based on environmental conditions. For each round of movement, any creature that could perceive you rolls a Perception trial against your Stealth Mastery DC. Alertness (Danger Sense) counters Sneaking. Each success reduces your Concealed by 1 tier. Success by 5 or more reduces your Concealed by 2 tiers instead.

<span id="disguise"></span>**Disguise:** Alter your appearance or impersonate others. Anyone who looks at you closely rolls a Perception trial against your Stealth mastery level + Charisma tier. Alertness (Discernment) counters Disguise. The GM may give them Dominance if they know the person you are impersonating well, or Impotence if they have no reason to doubt you.

<span id="opportunist"></span>**Opportunist:** On your turn, you can make an Opportunist attack for 2 AP. It is a single weapon attack using a Physique weapon that adds 2 Stealth mastery dice to the attack roll, and it gains the flanking bonus whatever opened it. You can make one when:

- you are flanking your target (melee or Enfilade only);
- 2 or more allies threaten your target (melee or ranged);
- your target is [Impaired](../core/conditions.md#impaired "Perception trial to resist. Stunning, disorientation, or delayed reflexes. Impaired from a failed Startle trial feeds into this chain. Tiers compound from T1 to T2; T3 transforms to total shutdown. T1 — Dazed: Penalty -1 to all rolls. Must act in Slow phase. T2 — Stunned: Penalty -2 to all rolls. Inferiority on attack rolls, defense rolls, and spell damage rolls. Must act in Slow phase. T3 — Paralyzed: Cannot take any actions. Considered Helpless and Catatonic. The creature is aware of its surroundings but unable to act or respond.") from a failed Startle trial; or
- you are Tier 1 [Concealed](../core/conditions.md#concealed "Perception trial to detect. Concealed measures how well others know where a creature is. It comes from two kinds of source: Tiers: Obscured, Shrouded, Hidden.") or higher from your target.

<span id="sneak-attack"></span>**Sneak attack.** If you are [Concealed](../core/conditions.md#concealed "Perception trial to detect. Concealed measures how well others know where a creature is. It comes from two kinds of source: Tiers: Obscured, Shrouded, Hidden.") from your target, your Opportunist attack can begin with a movement up to your speed toward it. Close quarters does not reveal you until the attack resolves.

<span id="basic-stealth-ability"></span>**Basic: Stealth Ability (choose one)**
Choose one of the following. Unchosen abilities become available at Expert and Grand Master.

### Environmental Specialization

Choose one environment: Wilderness, Urban, or Underground. Vanish no longer has Impotence in that environment, however creatures have Impotence on Perception trials to detect you while Sneaking. Wilderness covers forests, plains, mountains, and natural terrain. Urban covers cities, towns, buildings, and crowded areas. Underground covers dungeons, caves, sewers, and subterranean spaces.

### Nightwalker

In complete darkness, you can navigate and act without penalty by sensing creatures and objects within 10 feet through sound and air displacement. This is not magical detection — it relies on physical cues (breathing, movement, solid obstacles). Does not detect invisible creatures or objects that make no sound.

### Vanish

In-combat stealth. You must break line of sight before using this ability. Spend 1 AP and roll Physique tier die + Stealth mastery die - Worn Armor and Shield Encumbrance with Impotence against the encounter DC (Division + 1 of the highest-Division creature present). Success advances your [Concealed](../core/conditions.md#concealed "Perception trial to detect. Concealed measures how well others know where a creature is. It comes from two kinds of source: Tiers: Obscured, Shrouded, Hidden.") by 1 tier. Success by 5 or more advances your Concealed by 2 tiers.

<span id="skilled-opportunist-ability"></span>**Skilled: Opportunist Ability (choose one)**
Choose one of the following. Unchosen abilities become available at Master and Grand Master.

### Armor Bypass

When making an Opportunist attack, you can forfeit your flanking bonus to also use either Finding the Gap or Threading the Needle.

### Dirty Fighting

When making an Opportunist attack, you can forfeit your flanking bonus to force the target to succeed on a Strength trial against your Stealth Mastery DC or advance [Restricted](../core/conditions.md#restricted "Strength trial to resist. Grappling, binding, entanglement, or physical constraint. Tiers compound. T1 — Grappled: Cannot move. Penalty -1 to physical rolls. T2 — Pinned: Cannot move. Penalty -2 to physical rolls. Inferiority on attack and defense rolls. T3 — Restrained: Cannot move or take any actions except attempts to resist. Considered Helpless. Can maintain concentration. Can cast spells without somatic components (+1 magan).") by 1 tier.

### Ambuscade

When your target is [Impaired](../core/conditions.md#impaired "Perception trial to resist. Stunning, disorientation, or delayed reflexes. Impaired from a failed Startle trial feeds into this chain. Tiers compound from T1 to T2; T3 transforms to total shutdown. T1 — Dazed: Penalty -1 to all rolls. Must act in Slow phase. T2 — Stunned: Penalty -2 to all rolls. Inferiority on attack rolls, defense rolls, and spell damage rolls. Must act in Slow phase. T3 — Paralyzed: Cannot take any actions. Considered Helpless and Catatonic. The creature is aware of its surroundings but unable to act or respond.") from a failed Startle trial, you can forfeit your flanking bonus to make your Opportunist attack in place of a Charge's attack. You pay the Charge's 2 AP, not the Opportunist's.

<span id="expert-stealth-ability"></span>**Expert: Stealth Ability**
Choose one of the remaining Stealth Abilities you have not yet selected.

<span id="master-opportunist-ability"></span>**Master: Opportunist Ability**
Choose one of the Opportunist Abilities you have not yet selected.

<span id="grand-master-stealth-ability-and-opportunist-ability"></span>**Grand Master: Stealth Ability and Opportunist Ability**
You gain the remaining Stealth Ability and Opportunist Ability you have not yet selected.
