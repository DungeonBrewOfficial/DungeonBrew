# Legacy Items

*Five artifacts for Shadow of the Dragon Queen, each built for one character: a wizard, a cleric of Mishakal, a fighter, a paladin, and a kender. Every item belonged to a figure of legend, and every one reveals itself slowly.*

> **When I handed them out.** The Armor of the Blue Lady was found in the Tomb of Heroes at the end of Chapter 4. The others came around the end of Chapter 4 and the start of Chapter 5. There's a lot of travel in the Wastes, and I let characters study their items as part of daily travel, so by the end of the campaign every item was fully unlocked.

> **Rarity.** All five are artifacts: unique relics of legendary figures, well beyond ordinary legendary items. Each stat block shows the item fully unlocked.

## Unlocking an Item

A character must study, pray in, or train with their item to discover its features. Each item is divided into aspects, and each aspect hides one feature.

The player sees only each aspect's description. What it does stays a mystery until it's unlocked, so they choose what to study by what catches their eye: the hood, the clasps, the strange red gloop on a spoon.

For each day the character spends 8 hours with the item, they make one check (listed on each item) toward one aspect of their choice and add the result to that aspect's running total. When an aspect reaches 100 points, its feature is revealed and becomes active.

> *GM tip:* Give the player a handout listing only the aspects and their descriptions, and fill in each feature as it unlocks.

With a +5 modifier, an aspect takes about a week of study. An item with nine or ten aspects takes most of a campaign.

## Robes of Magius

*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a sorcerer, warlock, or wizard)*  
**Weight** 4 lb. · **Unidentified:** Dense crimson wool robes with intricate patterns in golden thread.

**Unlocking:** Study the robes; Intelligence (Arcana) check.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Material** | The body of the robes is luxurious crimson wool, densely woven, smooth and resilient. | You have resistance to cold damage, and you're unharmed by temperatures as low as −50 °F. |
| **Stitching** | The seams are meticulously double-stitched in strong, dark red silk thread. | Your AC equals 13 + your Dexterity modifier. |
| **Patterns** | The hems are embroidered with intricate arcane symbols and runes in contrasting gold thread. | You gain a +1 bonus to spell attack rolls and to your spell save DC. |
| **Lining** | The lining is fine, lightweight silk in a deeper red, with discreet diamond quilting. | Once per week, as an action, you can cure yourself of one poison or disease. |
| **Collar and Cuffs** | The collar stands upright, stiffened with a narrow band of leather. The cuffs are wide and turned back, showing gold embroidery inside and out, and trimmed with red velvet. | Once per day, you can make a push attack against a creature you can see within 60 feet, using your spell attack bonus. On a hit, the target takes 6d8 force damage and is pushed 15 feet away from you. |
| **Closure** | The robes fasten down the front with a row of brass clasps, each shaped like a small dragon. | Each of the 12 clasps can be detached and thrown as a single *magic missile* dart, dealing 1d4 + 1 force damage. You can throw up to 6 as an action. The robes regain 1d2 clasps each dawn. While all 12 are gone, you lose the Material feature until at least 4 are restored. |
| **Belt** | A wide, supple belt of deep mahogany leather, embossed with interwoven vines and arcane symbols touched with gold leaf. The polished brass buckle is shaped like an ancient rune and set with a small garnet. | The belt functions as a *staff of the python*. |
| **Pockets** | Several pockets are hidden in the lining, stitched so they can't be seen. | The robes have 6 extradimensional pockets. Each can hold up to 1 cubic foot of material, weighing no more than 10 pounds. |
| **Hood** | The hood is the same crimson wool lined with deep red silk, large enough to shelter the face, with a slight point at the back and an edge embroidered in gold thread. | While the hood is raised, you have darkvision out to 60 feet. If you already have darkvision, its range increases by 30 feet. |

## Armor of the Blue Lady

*Armor (half plate), artifact (requires attunement by a cleric of Mishakal)*  
**AC** 15 + Dex modifier (max 2), +2 · **Stealth** disadvantage · **Weight** 40 lb.  
**Unidentified:** A suit of armor reinforced with a strange metal.

**Unlocking:** Pray in the armor; Intelligence (Religion) check.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Aketon** | A padded jacket of quilted blue silk worn beneath the armor, reinforced with golden stitching. | When you spend Hit Dice during a short rest, you regain the maximum hit points for each die. |
| **Cuirass** | Chest and back plates of golden steel, etched with intricate patterns, with the symbol of Mishakal at the center. | The armor gains a +2 bonus to AC. |
| **Fauld** | Horizontal bands of golden steel protecting the lower torso and hips, decorated with blue enamel. | Your speed increases by 10 feet, and you ignore difficult terrain. |
| **Arm Harness** | Golden steel plates protecting the upper arms, linked with flexible joints. | Your weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage on a hit. |
| **Vambraces** | Forearm guards of tightly fitted golden steel plates. | You have advantage on checks made to climb and swim. |
| **Pauldrons** | Large shoulder guards engraved with the holy symbol of Mishakal and inlaid with blue sapphires. | As an action, you can speak a command word to sprout a pair of feathered wings for 1 hour or until you repeat the command word. You have a flying speed of 60 feet while the wings last. After each use, you must wait 1d12 hours before using them again. |
| **Gauntlets** | Plated gloves lined with blue-dyed leather. | When you cast a healing spell that requires you to touch the target, the target regains an extra 1d8 hit points. |
| **Barbut** | A helmet covering the head and face, leaving only the eyes visible, with blue sapphires around the brow and a golden crest. | You are immune to critical hits. |
| **Camail** | A chainmail curtain hanging from the helmet over the neck and shoulders, with golden rings and blue enamel. | You have advantage on saving throws against spells and effects with verbal components. |

## Huma's Greatsword

*Weapon (greatsword), artifact (requires attunement)*  
**Damage** 2d6 slashing, +2 · **Weight** 6 lb.  
**Unidentified:** A magnificent sword.

**Unlocking:** Train with the sword; Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check.

**The Blade** is high-quality dwarven steel, with an undulating pattern that shimmers in the light.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Edge** | Sharpened to a keen edge that never seems to dull. | You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. |
| **Fuller** | The central groove running the length of the blade is inlaid with adamantine. | When you score a critical hit, roll percentile dice. On a 91 or higher, you decapitate the target if it has at least one head. |
| **Ricasso** | The unsharpened section near the hilt, wrapped in black leather etched with the symbols of the good pantheon. | You can't be disarmed while wielding this weapon with two hands. |
| **Tip** | Forged of adamantine; the fuller runs to about 4 inches from the end before the blade widens to the point. | The weapon deals an extra 2d6 piercing damage on a critical hit. |

**The Hilt** is built on a continuous tang, so the sword feels solid and responsive. Inlays of gold, silver, and platinum run through the metalwork.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Grip** | Wrapped in the same black leather as the ricasso, braided for a reliable hold. | While wielding this weapon, your Strength score is 19, or increases by 2 if it's already 19 or higher. |
| **Quillons** | An adamantine crossguard shaped like a dragon's two wings. | You can use your reaction to parry a melee attack, gaining a +2 bonus to AC against it. |
| **Peen Block** | A rigid platinum spike shaped like a single flame, rising from the pommel. | If you parry an attack successfully, you can make one weapon attack against your attacker, dealing 1d4 bludgeoning damage. |
| **Pommel** | A heavy orb of adamantine, weighted to balance the blade. | Your Constitution score increases by 2. |

**The Scabbard** is hardened leather dyed deep burgundy, swirled with brass filigree and finished with a polished brass tip.

| Aspect | Feature |
|---|---|
| **Scabbard** | When you use Second Wind, you always regain the maximum number of hit points. |

## Huma's Dragonlance

*Weapon (lance), artifact (requires attunement)*  
**Damage** 1d12 piercing, +2 · **Properties** reach, special · **Weight** 6 lb.  
**Unidentified:** A magnificent lance.

> This is not the module's *dragonlance* item. It's a separate artifact: Huma's own lance.

**Unlocking:** Train with the lance; Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Spearhead** | Forged from high-quality steel and sharpened to a fine point. | The weapon deals an extra 3d6 force damage to dragons. |
| **Blade** | Just below the spearhead, the same steel, slightly serrated in the shape of the ridges along a dragon's back. | You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls with this weapon. |
| **Langets** | Metal strips shaped like dragon scales run down the sides of the shaft to protect it from being cut. | You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wielding this weapon. |
| **Grip** | Rich boar's hide, stitched tightly around the haft. | While wielding this weapon, your Strength score is 19, or increases by 2 if it's already 19 or higher. |
| **Crossguard** | Polished brass in the shape of dragon wings. | The weapon can be thrown, with a range of 60/180 feet. |
| **Haft** | Solid ash, carved and smoothed, with an oil finish that brings out the grain. | Once per day, as a bonus action, you can spend one Hit Die and regain hit points equal to the roll. |
| **Butt Spike** | A counterweight forged from the same steel as the spearhead. | When you attack with the lance, you can use a bonus action to strike with the butt spike, dealing 1d6 piercing damage. Butt spike attacks deal an extra die of damage on a critical hit. |
| **Lanyard** | A hand strap of braided silk, dyed deep crimson. | As a bonus action, you can call the lance back to your hand if it's within 1 mile of you. |
| **Pennon** | Plain white silk, until the wielder understands it. Then it bears the emblem of their deity or house. | The range of one aura you control is doubled. |

> *At my table:* The butt spike originally relied on a feat the character had. The bonus action strike above makes it work without one.

## Uncle Trapspringer's Silver Spoon of Turning

*Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a kender)*  
**Weight** 1 lb. · **Unidentified:** An elegant silver spoon. There's still some jam on the tip.

**Unlocking:** Study the spoon; Wisdom (Perception) check.

| Aspect | Description | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Handle** | Simple, elegant, and only slightly tarnished. | You have resistance to necrotic damage. |
| **Stem** | Slightly curved, and quite thin where it meets the shoulders. | Your Sneak Attack deals one extra die of damage. |
| **Shoulders** | Where the stem meets the bowl, subtly flared for a sturdy transition. | When you deal Sneak Attack damage, you gain temporary hit points equal to the Sneak Attack damage dealt. |
| **Bowl** | Shallow and polished, with a faint red mark that looks like strawberry jam. | Once per day, as an action, you can sense the presence of undead within 60 feet of you. |
| **Tip** | Arched sharply, for those pesky angles in a jam jar. | The tip functions as a *gem of brightness* and regains 1d6 charges each dawn. |
| **Red Gloop** | A small red... something near the tip of the bowl. It looks like strawberry jam, but it could be blood. | Once per day, as an action, you can Turn Undead as a cleric. The save DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier. |
| **Monogram** | "T.S.," delicately inscribed near the base of the handle. | Once per month, you can cast *revivify*. |
| **Engraving** | Small vines weave along the handle, stem, shoulders, and bowl. | Roll 1d6 at dawn. You can cast the matching spell once that day: 1, *death ward*; 2, *dimension door*; 3, *dispel evil and good*; 4, *prismatic spray*; 5, *sunburst*; 6, your choice of one spell from this list. |
| **Filigree** | Fine gold wire filigree running along the edges of the handle. | Once per day, you can cast *protection from evil and good*. |
