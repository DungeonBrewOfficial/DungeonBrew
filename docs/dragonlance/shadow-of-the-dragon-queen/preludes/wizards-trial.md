# Prelude: The Wizard's Trial

*A solo prelude for Shadow of the Dragon Queen, adapted from the module's "Eye in the Sky" prelude. The trial room and the Barb setting come from the book; Rovina's dialogue, the rival candidates, the timed ranking, and the tiered rewards are mine. Read-aloud text has been rewritten rather than reproduced.*

> **Reconstructed values.** Numbers and names marked **[R]** were not in my original notes and have been filled in for this write-up.

> **Structure.** The candidate faces a single trial room. They are ranked against three other candidates by how long they take to finish, and the rank sets the reward.

---

## The Barb

The Barb comes into view: a broken spire of gray stone, older than the Cataclysm, where those hoping to join the Mages of High Sorcery come to prove themselves. Most nights it is a ruin and nothing more. Tonight is the Night of the Eye. Krynn's three moons have aligned, magic has woken in the old stones, and a soft glow spills from the archway.

Inside, flaming sconces light a broad atrium hung with tapestries of the three moons. Doors ring the room, and moonlight falls through tall windows overhead. A gray-haired woman in red robes waits at the center, calm and unhurried. Several other candidates are already here: two human women and a Silvanesti man. All three seem absorbed in their own thoughts. The red-robed woman nods for you to take your place with the others.

*Ask the player to describe their character.*

## Rovina's Instructions

> "I am Rovina, resident and caretaker of the Barb. Tonight, each of you must face a trial in one of the tower's rooms. This trial can only be taken tonight. Each of you has begun your path toward the Test. This is not the Test, only a trial. There is no danger tonight, as there will be with the Test, but you must succeed here to move forward. I will be watching to see how efficient, how clever, and how adept you are in your magical talents. Each of you may ask one question, if you have it."

**Odessa Varn [R]:** "What happens if we do not complete the trial?"

> "If you do not complete your trial before the end of the night, the rooms will vanish and you will be expelled from them. You may not attempt the trial again for 547 days, when the moons next align to allow entrance."

**Brienne Halloway [R]:** "What is the nature of the trial?"

> "To test your magical aptitude."

**Brienne:** "I meant..."

> "You have had your question."

**Ilthanis Sarethiel [R]:** "Why must we take the trial before taking the Test?"

> "In the past, not so long ago even, initiates were permitted to bypass the trial and proceed to the Test. However, we have found that fewer initiates die during the Test if the trial is required."

**The character's question:** *(the player asks, and Rovina answers as the GM sees fit)*

> "Very good. Choose a door and we will proceed."

## The Trial Room

Beyond the door is a round chamber floored in black stone, its walls crowded with carved runes. Nothing stands in it but a pedestal at the center, where an ornate key rests balanced on its point.

The door closes behind you and is gone. Across the room, a matching door has appeared, shut tight.

### Running the Room

The rotunda is 100 feet across. The exit door is locked. It opens with the key on the pedestal or with a *knock* spell.

**The Invisible Maze.** An invisible, shifting maze fills the floor and reaches to the ceiling. It is 90 feet across, leaving a 5-foot walkway between its outer edge and the rotunda wall. Anyone who moves more than 5 feet toward the pedestal walks into an unseen wall.

- *Detect magic* shows the walls glowing with illusion and transmutation magic.
- Effects that reveal invisible things, such as *faerie fire* or *see invisibility*, make the walls visible. With the walls visible, the maze is easy to navigate.
- Without a way to see the walls, a candidate can still feel their way through. Each attempt takes 1 hour and requires a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Investigation) check. A failed attempt can be repeated after another hour.

**The Wall Carvings.** The runes around the rotunda are an arcane cipher for the *knock* spell. A candidate deciphers them with a successful DC 14 Intelligence (Arcana) check or after an hour of study. Once deciphered, the wall can be read like a spell scroll of *knock*. It is not used up, and casting the spell opens the exit door, completing the trial.

**The Golden Key.** Touching the key makes the maze walls vanish. The key opens the exit door and disappears once used.

### Timing the Trial [R]

Most of the room can be solved in a minute or two by a competent caster, so time is tracked in minutes:

| Action | Time |
|---|---|
| Casting a spell or making a successful Arcana check | 1 minute |
| Crossing the maze with the walls visible | 1 minute |
| Casting a spell as a ritual | 10 minutes |
| A failed Arcana check (forcing an hour of study) or each Investigation attempt | 60 minutes |

The other three candidates finish at fixed times:

| Candidate | Finishes after |
|---|---|
| Ilthanis Sarethiel | 5 minutes |
| Odessa Varn | 15 minutes |
| Brienne Halloway | 70 minutes |

The character's place is wherever their total falls among these. Ties go to the character. In practice, a clean solve takes 1st, a ritual casting drops to 2nd, and a single hour of fumbling lands 3rd.

## Conclusion

You pass through the door to find Rovina and the other candidates waiting patiently for you.

> Rovina smiles warmly. "Well done. I am glad to see that you have finished your trial."

Her response depends on the character's place:

- **1st:** "Most impressive."
- **2nd:** "You did well, but that was expected."
- **3rd:** "From what I had heard, I had expected you to finish the trial more quickly."
- **4th:** "It is better to have finished than to have failed."

Rovina hands you an ornate sealed scroll, along with your reward.

> "I understand you are on your way to Vogler. Near it is the city of Kalaman. You must keep your scroll sealed and hand it to a mage of the Black Robes by the name of Wyhan, who resides within the city. The contents are meant for her eyes alone."

### Rewards

| Place | Reward |
|---|---|
| 1st | **Tome of Recovery**, plus 4 spell scrolls of your choice, 3rd level or lower |
| 2nd | 4 spell scrolls of your choice, 2nd level or lower |
| 3rd | 3 spell scrolls of your choice, 2nd level or lower |
| 4th | 2 spell scrolls of your choice, 2nd level or lower |

## Homebrew Items

### Tome of Recovery
*Wondrous item, rare*  
**Weight** 5 lb.

The cover of this book bears a relief, carved in bronze-painted wood, of what looks like a man of great wisdom.

This book contains intuition and insight exercises, and its words are charged with magic. If you spend 48 hours over a period of 6 days or fewer studying the book's contents and practicing its guidelines, your Arcane Recovery can be used twice per day and recovers 1 additional level of spell slots. The book then loses its magic, but regains it in a century.
