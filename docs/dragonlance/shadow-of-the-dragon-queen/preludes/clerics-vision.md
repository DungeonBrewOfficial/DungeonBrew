# Prelude: The Cleric's Vision (Mishakal)

*A solo prelude for Shadow of the Dragon Queen, built on the skeleton of the module's "Broken Silence" prelude (a battlefield vision, an object clutched by the fallen, a hidden temple, a statue of the character's god). The prose, the branching choice, and the rewards are mine.*

> **Structure.** One vision, one choice: go to the dying man or go to the glimmer. Each branch carries its own reward.

> **GM note on the gods.** The campaign is set before the gods of Krynn have openly returned. This prelude, like the paladin's (Habbakuk), has the god tell the character to keep their return hidden until "one in the south" brings word (Goldmoon, in Mishakal's case). It's there to justify the character's divine power while reminding the player that miracles shouldn't be on public display yet. Mishakal calls the character "daughter"; adjust as needed.

---

## The Camp

A small camp, removed from any searching eyes, already dims in the dark. The embers glow softly, like small lights beneath the red glow of Lunitari. The chill of the night is mild, and the figure lying beside the fire hardly needs it. Their eyes flutter softly in the last moments before sleep takes them for the evening.

*Ask the player to describe their character.*

## The Vision of Mishakal

You find yourself standing in the midst of a battlefield, the air thick with the metallic tang of blood and the cries of the fallen. The sky above is a tumultuous grey. Dragons of myriad colors battle with fire and lightning and death. A rending, horrendous shriek is followed by a smattering rain of blood, the globules coating you. The chaos of the sky mirrors the chaos around you: men and dwarves locked in battle with ogres, elves falling beneath the grim, hooked swords of dragon-faced monstrosities, and the cries of those nearing their final moments.

Amidst the madness, your attention is drawn to a wounded soldier lying a short distance away. He is one of dozens like him, but for some reason your eye goes to him. His breaths are shallow, his eyes filled with a plea for help as he reaches a shaking hand toward you. But beyond him, something flashes, catching the light. Half-buried beneath a heap of corpses, something is there, glimmering with an otherworldly glow.

**Go to the man, or go to the glimmer?**

---

### Go to the Man

You go to the man's side, plying your skills as a healer as best you can. You know as soon as you kneel beside him that there is no hope of saving him. You try to comfort him, to offer him peace. You whisper the prayers of Mishakal, the way you heard your parents whisper them to the sick and dying they could not save. The words never felt like much before, just words, but now they ring in your ears. The man reaches to clasp your hand, and you take it. Anything to give him comfort before the end. The soft touch of vellum fills your palm.

You wake. The sky is still dark. You feel something in your hand. Vellum. A crude map is drawn on its surface.

*The map leads to a forgotten temple.*

You take a few moments to examine the map. It is strange: no scale, random topography with no towns or cities, and only a small, faded X near the center, not far from a crude road and what appears to be a stand of trees. You cannot make sense of it, so you gather your gear to continue your journey. Setting off down the road, you take the map out again and see that the X has not only moved but has grown less faded. You watch it as you travel and soon realize that the X is you, and it grows thicker and darker as you go. When it begins to fade, you turn back. Eventually you find yourself off the path, trudging through the woods, until you come upon the entrance to an overgrown temple. Tucking the map away, you go inside.

Its dilapidated walls and broken statues whisper of a time when the gods walked among mortals. The air is thick with a sense of ancient power and forgotten lore. As you approach, a familiarity tugs at your soul, an unspoken invitation from a long-lost friend. Further inside, the temple holds remnants of a grandeur now lost to time. In a larger chamber stands a central statue, eroded yet majestic, dimly lit by a stark ray of Solinari's light falling through the broken ceiling. It is the likeness of Mishakal.

As you pause before her in awe, the stone lips part and she speaks:

> "I am coming, daughter, as quickly as I may. Soon you will hear tell of my return. There is one in the south who brings word, and until then you must keep my return hidden from all but a few whom you trust. You must be my candle against the coming night. Henceforth, you bear my mark."

She reaches out and lightly touches your cheek. A sense of divine wisdom fills you, and your eyes are alight with understanding.

**Reward:** 1 additional use of **Channel Divinity**.

---

### Go to the Glimmer

The man is in need, but there are dozens like him covering the battlefield. You could go to him, but what of the hundreds or thousands still yet to die? You need to help the living, if you can. Perhaps whatever you saw glinting ahead is a weapon to use against the enemy.

Closing the distance, you drop to your knees and heave aside a dead body. You try not to look at its face. Beneath it you find an amulet bearing the symbol of Mishakal. Whatever glow or glimmer you thought you saw is gone. Instead, the amulet is covered in blood, still clasped in the hand of... you pause, realizing that the adorned corpse bears your own likeness, its eyes staring blankly at nothing. Shakily, you reach up to close its eyes. Shivering at that sightless gaze, you feel your own fingertips touch your own eyelids. You turn your attention back to the amulet and pry it from your hand.

You wake. The sky is still dark. You feel something in your hand. A solid object. The amulet. Pristine, with no blood on it now, but the symbol of Mishakal still upon it.

The dream still haunts you, and you are wary of the object, but it is warm to the touch, comforting, and eventually you decide it will cause you no harm. When you place it around your neck, you hear a soft whisper in your ear:

> "I am coming, daughter, as quickly as I may. Soon you will hear tell of my return. There is one in the south who brings word, and until then you must keep my return hidden from all but a few whom you trust. I grant you this symbol to bear in remembrance of what is to come, and of what awaits should we fail. I am with you."

**Reward:** **Amulet of Mishakal**

## Homebrew Items

### Amulet of Mishakal
*Adventuring gear, holy symbol*  
**Cost** 500 gp · **Weight** 1 lb.

This amulet is made of platinum and embedded with a gold relief of Mishakal.

Only a devotee of Mishakal can use the amulet. Once per short rest, the wearer can cast *cure wounds* at 1st level without expending a spell slot.
