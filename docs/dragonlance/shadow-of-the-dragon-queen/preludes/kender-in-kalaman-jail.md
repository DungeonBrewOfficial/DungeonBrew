# Prelude: The Kender in Kalaman Jail

*A solo prelude for Shadow of the Dragon Queen. Written for a kender player who joined the campaign late, so it hands out more rewards than the other preludes.*

> **Reconstructed values.** Numbers marked **[R]** were not in my original notes and have been filled in for this write-up. Adjust to taste.

> **Structure.** The player picks one jail companion (dwarf, elf, or kender), then one area to explore (guard room or solo cell).

> **Sourcing.** Items marked *(XGtE)* are from *Xanathar's Guide to Everything* and are referenced by name only. Everything else is homebrew.

---

## The Kalaman City Jail

The city jail of Kalaman is abuzz with the usual commotion. The guards, exasperated by the constant presence of kender, largely ignore them, along with the assortment of detainees held for misdemeanors. They patrol lazily and intermittently, ignoring minor nuisances. The jail is not large: two sizeable holding cells along one wall and a row of smaller single cells along the other. There is only one exit, and it passes the guard room.

*Ask the player to describe their character.*

## The Chaos Begins

You are deposited into your cell and the guard takes his keys back out of your hands. You mutter, "Oh! You must have dropped those. You're welcome!" He cuffs you on the back of the head, locks the cell, and walks away.

Glancing about, you are immediately overwhelmed by interesting people to talk to: a dwarf drunkenly singing songs and unwittingly leading a choir of kender; a nude elf, apparently meditating, while several kender attempt headstands nearby; and of course a mob of kender who immediately assail you with introductions, "How do you dos" and "What's your names," all while rummaging through your pockets.

Would you like to introduce yourself to the dwarf, the elf, or the kender?

---

### The Dwarf

**Limgig**, arrested for public drunkenness, sings bawdy songs and tells tall tales.

**Sing Along.** Join Limgig in song. The song is "Barkeep's Advice," from *More Leaves from the Inn of the Last Home* (p. 186). While he's in full voice, a **DC 15 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check lifts his flask.

- *Item:* **Limgig's Flask**

**Listen to Tales.** Listen closely and draw him out with a **DC 12 [R] Charisma (Persuasion)** check. On a success, you learn of several adventurers in the area looking to join a band:

- Andir Valmakos, the Mage
- Ayik Ur, the Archer
- Hrigg Roundrook, the Warrior
- Iriad, the Expert
- Levna Drakehorn, the Knight
- Tem Temble, the Wanderer

**Reward:** Interception die increases to **1d12**.

---

### The Elf

**Thalindra**, arrested for public nudity while performing a nature ritual, meditates and performs strange exercises.

**Meditate with Thalindra.** You have advantage on one roll of your choice while in jail.

**Join in the Exercises.** Make a **DC 12 [R] Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check to keep up. Partway through the routine, Thalindra pops out her glass eye to wipe the sweat from the socket and sets it on the bench beside her. A **DC 20 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand)** check palms it before she notices.

- *Item:* **Ersatz Eye** *(XGtE)*

**Reward:** Second Wind increases to **1d12**.

> *GM note:* As written, an ersatz eye can't be removed by anyone other than its owner, which is why she takes it out herself. Advantage from meditating applies to the Sleight of Hand roll if the player does both.

**Pippin**, in for "borrowing" a city official's hat, fidgets constantly and finds endless ways to entertain himself.

**Exchange Stories.** Swap tales of adventure. You end up with a random assortment of "borrowed" trinkets. Roll on the Kender Pouches table at the end of the prelude.

## Breaking Out (Into More Chaos)

When the guards bring lunch, you suddenly find yourself holding the guard's keys. Fully intending to return them, you open the cell. Do you head for the guard room or check out the solo cells?

### The Guard Room

The guards keep a makeshift lounge inside the cell block and often leave things behind.

**Sneak In.** You find a forgotten meal and a ring of rusty keys. None of them fit any lock in the jail, though one has a question mark worked into its head. A lock hangs open on the cabinet door.

- *Items:* **Mystery Key** *(XGtE)*, **Lock of Trickery** *(XGtE)*

**Scavenge.** You turn up a small pouch of questionable herbs, a half-written love letter, and a quiver of crossbow bolts with oddly heavy heads.

- *Item:* **Walloping Crossbow Bolts (20)** *(XGtE, walloping ammunition)*

### The Solo Cell

A notorious thief, currently asleep, is locked in one of the solo cells.

**Chat While They're Awake.** The thief teaches you a secret knock that supposedly opens any door, then challenges you to a game of dice. You leave with their die.

- *Item:* **Charlatan's Die** *(XGtE)*

**Scavenge While They Sleep.** You find a lockpick set missing crucial pieces, a pair of boots with runes on the soles, and a short rod that turns out to be much longer than it looks.

- *Items:* **Boots of False Tracks** *(XGtE)*, **Pole of Collapsing** *(XGtE)*

---

## Conclusion

After two weeks of chaotic interactions, random acquisitions, and new, if temporary, friends, your time in jail comes to an end. The guards, relieved to see you go, return your belongings and escort you out. You step back into the city of Kalaman with your pockets full of trinkets and treasures, ready for the next adventure.

**Final Inventory:** Roll **1d4 + 1 [R]** times on the Kender Pouches table.

## Kender Pouches (d200)

> **Using it all campaign.** I didn't retire this table after the prelude. Any time the kender passed through a crowd, they picked up things from it and lost something of their own.

**In a crowd**, the kender makes a Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check. The result sets how many times to roll on the table **[R]**:

| Check result | Rolls on the table |
|---|---|
| 9 or lower | 0 |
| 10–14 | 1 |
| 15–19 | 2 |
| 20–24 | 3 |
| 25 or higher | 1d4 + 2 |

The kender finds the items in a pocket later. Whatever the roll, the GM also picks one of the kender's carried items at random (never anything equipped or attuned) **[R]**. It's gone, and the kender has no idea when it went missing.

*Optional:* On a natural 1, someone notices, and the kender is chased out of the area.

Entries marked *(DMG)* point to the treasure tables in the *Dungeon Master's Guide*. Entries marked *(live)* are creatures, using the standard stat blocks.
