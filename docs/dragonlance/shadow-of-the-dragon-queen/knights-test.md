# The Knight's Test

*A test of knighthood for a Solamnic squire in Shadow of the Dragon Queen. Run it at the end of Chapter 4, after the party has finished its work in Kalaman and before it leaves for the Wastes, alongside the wizard's test. Designed for level 4.*

> **Setting note.** The scene opens in Castle Kalaman and uses two module NPCs, Darrett Highwater and Marshal Vendris. If you're running it elsewhere, swap them out.

> **Structure.** Three trials worth 25 points, plus up to 5 bonus points for honor in the duel. The squire's score sets the outcome: provisional knighthood below 10, full knighthood at 10, and gear rewards above that.

> **At my table.** The squire took the bracelet at Thrip, so the warhorse Karem moved here as the top reward. If your squire already has Karem, replace the 25-point reward with something else.

---

## Sir Gunthar Arrives

*The squire and Darrett Highwater are training in the courtyard of Castle Kalaman with soldiers in the city's service. Place other party members where it makes sense; at my table, the wizard sat to one side with Marshal Vendris, going over notes before another meeting with Wyhan.*

A small retinue of mounted knights rides in. Their leader is a stocky, grim man with steely gray hair.

> "Squire Highwater! Squire [name]! Present!
>
> "I understand it is your intent to become knights under the banner of Solamnia. Is that so?
>
> "Good! You will face three trials. First is the Trial of the Soul. You will spend the next three days in solitude, fasting and meditating on the Song of the Measure, showing your commitment and duty to the Oath and the Measure. Next is the Trial of the Mind. You will interpret the Song of the Measure, showing your faith and understanding of the Oath and the Measure. Finally, you will engage in the Trial of the Heart, showing your honor and courage in reflection of the Oath and the Measure.
>
> "Any questions?
>
> "Follow Knight uth Zarach. He will show you to your rooms, where you can begin your meditations."

---

## Trial of the Soul (10 points)

The squire spends three days fasting and three days in prayer. Each day, they make one save for each.

| Day | DC | Points for success |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | 1 |
| 2 | 13 | 2 |
| 3 | 16 | 2 |

Each half of the trial is worth up to 5 points.

### Fasting (Constitution saves)

Failed fasting saves add up:

| Failed CON saves | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | None |
| 2 | 1 level of exhaustion |
| 3 | 1 level of exhaustion, and the squire begins the Trial of the Heart at half hit points |

### Prayer (Wisdom saves)

Each successful prayer save lets the squire cross one decoy off the answer key before the Trial of the Mind. Meditating on the Song makes it easier to interpret. Failed prayer saves have no penalty beyond the lost point.

---

## Trial of the Mind (5 points)

Give the players the five verses of the Song of the Measure without their labels, along with the answer key. They match each verse to the virtue it describes. Each correct match is worth 1 point.

**Answer key:** Courage, Duty, Faith, Heroism, Honor, Justice, Loyalty, the Measure

Five are correct. Loyalty, Heroism, and Justice are decoys, drawn from the virtues of the Orders of the Crown, the Sword, and the Rose. Remove one decoy for each successful prayer save in the Trial of the Soul.

> *GM note:* At my table, the squire's player asked the rest of the party for help, and I allowed it. Allow it or don't, depending on the player.

## Trial of the Heart (10 points, plus honor)

The squire duels Sir Aldric uth Merren, a veteran of Sir Gunthar's retinue (use the **knight** stat block). The duel is fought until one combatant drops to 0 hit points, and all damage is nonlethal.

**Damage.** The squire earns 1 point for every 5 damage dealt, up to 10 points.

> *GM note:* This fight is not meant to be won. A knight has two attacks a round and a Parry reaction; a level 4 squire has one attack. Most squires will lose, and the trial measures how well they fight and how they carry themselves while doing it. Honor points are earned on top of the damage points and aren't limited by the damage cap.

### Aldric

Aldric treats a duel as a lesson. He talks throughout: dry, courteous, a little gruff. Play him as a person, not a test, so that his moments of restraint read as character rather than setup.

### Honor: The Reset (+3)

The first time Aldric takes a critical hit or drops below half his hit points, he staggers out of reach to recover, provoking an opportunity attack from the squire. If the squire holds their attack, they earn **3 honor**. This only happens once, and given the odds, it won't come up in most duels.

### Honor: The Yield (+2)

When the squire drops below 10 hit points, Aldric gives up his attack and offers the squire the chance to yield. If his first attack on a turn drops the squire below 10, he holds his second attack and makes the offer instead. Score the squire's response:

| Response | Honor |
|---|---|
| Accepts and shows Aldric respect | +2 |
| Accepts but is petulant or disrespectful | 0 |
| Declines, then waits for Aldric to set his guard before fighting on | +2 |
| Declines and attacks immediately, treating the offer as a free turn | 0 |

If the squire instead offers Aldric the chance to yield when *he* is below 10 hit points, Aldric accepts and the squire earns **+2 honor**. To make this possible, report Aldric's condition at half and near 10 hit points (see the condition lines below).

The squire can earn yield honor only once per duel.

### Sample Lines

*Opening*
> "Sir Gunthar wants your measure taken, and I mean to take it properly. Draw when you're ready."

*When the squire lands a good hit*
> "There. That one I felt."  
> "Good. You kept your feet under you."

*When Aldric lands a hit*
> "You watched my sword. Watch my shoulders."  
> "Breathe, squire. You're holding it."

*The reset*
> He staggers back, works his jaw, and spits. "Well struck." He gives ground to set his guard again.

*Condition (GM narration)*
> **At half hit points:** His breathing has gone ragged, and he's favoring his left side.  
> **Near 10 hit points:** His guard is sagging now. Each parry comes a little later than the last.

*The yield offer*
> Aldric lowers his blade a hand's width. "You've given me a fair fight. There's no shame in setting your sword down. Do you yield?"

*Responses*
> **Graceful acceptance:** "Well fought. Sir Gunthar will hear it from me."  
> **Petulant acceptance:** He says nothing, only looks to Sir Gunthar and shakes his head.  
> **Declines and resets:** "Then set your guard." He waits until the squire does.  
> **Declines and attacks immediately:** He takes the blow, then says flatly, "I see."  
> **The squire offers Aldric the yield:** He barks a laugh. "You'd let me keep my pride? Aye. I yield."

*If the squire knocks him out*
> When he comes to, still on his back, he looks up at the sky. "Well. That's settled, then."

## Rewards

Rewards are cumulative.

| Points | Reward |
|---|---|
| Under 10 | **Provisional knighthood.** Sir Gunthar knights the squire on the condition that a full knight vouches for them once they have proven themselves in the campaign ahead. The GM decides when that happens. |
| 10 | **Full knighthood**, and the **Solamnic Tabard** |
| 15 | **Solamnic Greatsword** |
| 20 | **Plate armor** |
| 25 | **Karem**, a warhorse |

## Items

### Solamnic Tabard
*Wondrous item, common*  
**Weight** 2 lb.

While you wear this tabard and aren't frightened, allies within 30 feet of you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

### Solamnic Greatsword
*Weapon (greatsword), common*  
**Damage** 2d6 + 1 slashing · **Properties** heavy, two-handed · **Weight** 6 lb.

A masterwork blade. Its craftsmanship grants a +1 bonus to damage rolls but no bonus to attack rolls. In darkness, the unsheathed blade sheds moonlight in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet.
