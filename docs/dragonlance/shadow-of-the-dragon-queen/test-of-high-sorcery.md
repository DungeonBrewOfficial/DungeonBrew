# The Test of High Sorcery

*A replacement for the module's provisional-membership handwave in Shadow of the Dragon Queen. Run it at the end of Chapter 4, when Wyhan calls the candidate back to her apothecary (see "Message from Wyhan"). Designed for a level 4 wizard; works for most arcane casters.*

> **Sourcing.** The setup (the sealed scroll, Wyhan's apothecary, the Scroll Predictions table) is from the module. Wyhan's briefing and the masters' framing were inspired by the Test scenes in the Dragonlance novels; all text here is rewritten. Reconstructed values are marked **[R]**.

> **Design intent.** In the novels, the candidate doesn't know the Test has begun. That's hard to manage with a player who knows a test is coming. So Wyhan tells the candidate exactly what the Test will contain, then says it happens in three days. It begins the moment the candidate turns to leave. The player knows what's in the Test, just not when it starts. Since the players expect Kalaman to be under attack, treating the *attack* as real is essential to selling it to the candidate.

> **The rest of the party.** Any other PCs nearby are caught in the illusion without knowing it. Don't tell them. Their panic sells the scene. Their choices don't count toward the candidate's robe, and anything they take vanishes when the illusion breaks. They don't see the candidate again for about four days.

## Wyhan's Briefing

> "So. You wish to be a mage. And the rest of you? Going to buy something?
>
> "Good. Now that your business is done, be gone. I have tower business to discuss with this initiate, and I do not need you clamoring about the place.
>
> *(Once the others have left.)* "Tell me, initiate, what do you know of your Test?
>
> "Less than you think. In three days you will stand in the Tower of High Sorcery in Wayreth Forest. Par-Salian of the White Robes will preside, with Justarius of the Red and Ladonna of the Black beside him. They will not be gentle. You will face at least three trials of your knowledge of magic, at least three that magic alone will not solve, and at least one opponent above your station. Some candidates never walk out of the Tower. The Conclave considers that a fair price. Are you prepared to die for your art, initiate?
>
> "Good. Come back in three days, and I will see you to Wayreth."

> *GM note:* Her list doubles as your blueprint. The trials below map onto it: magic (the glyphs, the scrolls, the spellbooks), beyond magic (the beam, Wyhan, the civilians), and the opponent (the draconian illusionist).

## The Test Begins

As you turn to leave, a loud explosion shakes the apothecary. The roof collapses, and flames engulf it.

**DC 20 Dexterity saving throw.**

**Success.** You dive out of the way as a large roof timber crashes down where you stood. Across the room, you hear Wyhan grunt and the heavy sound of her body hitting the floor. Flames and timbers block your way to her; she's out of sight on the other side of her counter. Through a hole in the roof, you can see the sky. You hear a loud roar, and screams.

**Failure.** You take 1d6 bludgeoning damage as the timber falls on you, pinning you to the floor. Fire spreads along the beam toward where you're trapped. Through the smoke, you can barely see the sky through the hole in the roof. You hear a loud roar, and screams.

### Pinned

A pinned candidate can get free by:

- calling out for help
- a DC 13 **[R]** Dexterity (Acrobatics) check to wriggle out
- a DC 15 **[R]** Strength (Athletics) check to push the beam off
- *unseen servant* to work the beam like a lever (advantage on the Athletics check)
- the pitcher of water nearby, reached with *unseen servant* or *shape water*, to slow the fire

### Wyhan

Wyhan is behind her counter, unconscious and dying. She makes death saving throws each round. On a natural 20, she comes to with 1 hit point and can help get herself out. The door behind her is locked, and the candidate is blocked from the other exits.

### The Glyphs

Three *glyphs of warding* protect the shop (save DC 14 **[R]**):

| Glyph | Location | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| *Suggestion* | Center of the room | Leave the room |
| *Explosive runes* | Rear of the room, centered | 5d8 cold damage |
| *Greater invisibility* | Left of the door; must be touched | Invisibility on the one who touches it |

The *suggestion* glyph is the cruel one: a candidate who fails the save walks out and leaves Wyhan behind.

### The Shop

**Scrolls on the table:** *passwall*, *dimension door*, *knock* (×3), *gust of wind*, *watery sphere*, *Tenser's floating disk*.

Several of these are above a level 4 wizard's reach, so casting them means an ability check (DC 10 + the spell's level). Choosing the right scroll, and knowing which ones you can actually cast, is part of the Test. *Tenser's floating disk* can carry Wyhan.

**Spellbooks:** three, one for each order.

| Book | Schools |
|---|---|
| White | Abjuration, Divination |
| Red | Transmutation, Illusion |
| Black | Necromancy, Enchantment |

**Staves and wands:** a rack of unlabeled items, and a temptation. Describe them by appearance only. As an action, a candidate can make a DC 13 **[R]** Intelligence (Arcana) check to learn what one item does. Every action spent identifying is an action not spent getting out.

Anything taken from the shop vanishes with the illusion, unless the Marks of the Test grant it as a boon.

| Staff | Description |
|---|---|
| *Staff of charming* | Pale ash wood, its head carved into an open hand. Oddly pleasant to hold. |
| *Staff of the adder* | Dark wood carved with scales, its head shaped like a serpent's. The wood feels faintly warm. |
| *Staff of the python* | A thick, banded staff, heavy for its size, the grain coiling around it like a body. |
| *Vicious* quarterstaff | A plain iron-shod staff, nicked and scarred, its end stained dark. |
| +2 quarterstaff | A smooth, unadorned staff, perfectly balanced. Lighter than it should be. |

| Wand | Description |
|---|---|
| *Wand of magic detection* | A slender glass rod that fogs faintly when breathed on. |
| *Wand of magic missiles* | Polished bone, the tip worn smooth as if it's been pointed often. |
| *Wand of secrets* | Gray wood with a keyhole carved into the handle. |
| *Wand of the war mage*, +1 | Black wood bound in red leather, the grip worn by use. |
| *Wand of web* | Wrapped in fine silvery threads that stick to the fingers. |
| *Wand of binding* | Iron, cold to the touch, ringed with tiny chain links. |
| *Wand of enemy detection* | Bronze, with a needle-thin tip that seems to twitch. |
| *Wand of fear* | Charred black wood that smells of smoke even away from the fire. |
| *Wand of paralysis* | Pale crystal, perfectly still in the hand, with no warmth to it at all. |
| *Wand of wonder* | Brightly painted in clashing colors, a different hue at every glance. |

Escaping the shop is possible with almost any combination of spells and items. It is more impressive with Wyhan in tow.

## The Street

Outside, Kalaman's market is under attack: draconians in the streets, a red dragon overhead, city guards fighting, civilians caught in the open. The Test ends when the candidate kills the draconian illusionist or is defeated.

**Encounter:** 6 baaz draconians, 1 draconian illusionist, 1 draconian mage, 1 draconian mastermind, 1 young red dragon, 8 guards (allies), and civilians: a child, a drunk, an elderly man, a messenger, and a refugee.

> *GM note:* The encounter is far beyond a level 4 character, on purpose. It's an illusion, and the candidate isn't meant to win the battle, only to face the illusionist and show what they do along the way. Use the civilians as choices: who the candidate protects, who they ignore, and who they use.

## Scoring the Robe

Each meaningful choice during the Test earns a mark toward Good, Neutral, or Evil. Examples **[R]**:

| Choice | Mark |
|---|---|
| Saves Wyhan, or tries to | Good |
| Leaves Wyhan behind | Evil |
| Takes staves or wands to fight with | Neutral |
| Takes staves or wands and flees | Evil |
| Shields civilians | Good |
| Ignores civilians to reach the illusionist | Neutral |
| Uses civilians as cover | Evil |
| Disarms a glyph, or turns one against the draconians | Neutral |

The majority suggests a robe: Good for White, Neutral for Red, Evil for Black. The spellbook the candidate took breaks ties.

The suggestion isn't final. The candidate may take the suggested robe or one step from it. Red sits between White and Black, so a candidate suggested for White may choose Red, but not Black. Robes in Krynn aren't fixed for life anyway because the novels have shown characters changing allegiance.

Afterward, compare the result with Wyhan's Scroll Predictions. A mismatch is worth a scene with her.

## Waking in Wayreth

You wake in a comfortable bed. A slight breeze comes through a nearby window, rustling the trees outside. Your body aches, your wounds only half-healed, if that. A sudden realization washes over you: you are in the Tower of High Sorcery in Wayreth. The memory is hard to grasp. Going to see Wyhan, the days that passed before returning, the moment she spirited you across Ansalon to the Tower, entering the Test and everything that happened within it. It all seemed so real that you could not tell it from reality.

Eventually, you are back on your feet and standing before the masters of the three orders who oversaw your Test: Par-Salian of the White, Justarius of the Red, and Ladonna of the Black.

> **Par-Salian** (frail, white-bearded, sharp blue eyes): "I am glad to see you on your feet again, Magius. For a time, I thought the Test had gotten the better of you, but I see you are made of sterner stuff than some would give you credit for."

> *GM note:* "Magius" is used here as a title for a full mage. In *The Soulforge*, Raistlin's first trial is writing "I, Magius." It's also the name of Huma's companion.

> **Ladonna** (iron-gray hair in intricate braids), smiling slightly at the jab: "I believe your Test was one of the more difficult I have seen, but you live to tell of it, and that is all that matters."

> **Justarius** (athletic, dark-eyed, walking on a leg crippled in his own Test): "It is my understanding that you intended to take the [robe] before entering the Test. Do you think your actions still warrant the [robe]?"

> **Ladonna:** "Let the initiate answer, Justarius. We have already seen what they do when they believe no one is watching."

Justarius's question is where the candidate argues for their robe, within one step of the suggestion.

## Marks of the Test

Every Test leaves a mark. The candidate gains one injury and its paired boon, based on what dealt the worst harm during the Test **[R]**.

| Cause | Injury | Boon |
|---|---|---|
| Crushed by the beam | **Cracked sternum.** Constant chest pain; disadvantage on Constitution saves against inhaled acid or poison. | A staff from the shop* |
| Burned by fire | **Burns.** Sensitive to heat and cold; take 1 extra damage per die of cold or fire damage. | Arcane Ward (as the School of Abjuration feature) |
| Immolated by the dragon | **Burns.** As above, plus disadvantage on saves against being frightened by dragons. | **Relived moments.** You relive each moment over and over. Gain Portent (as the School of Divination feature). |
| Stabbed by a draconian | **Festering wound.** Hit point maximum reduced by 1 per level. | The +1 *wand of the war mage* from the shop |
| Psychic damage from the illusionist | **Madness.** Roll an indefinite madness. *(My table used [Lingering Injuries & Insanity](../../supplements/lingering-injuries-and-insanity.md).)* | **Glassy eyes.** Your eyes shimmer like glass, and you are permanently under the effect of *detect magic*. |
| Poison spray | **Sickly.** Constitution reduced by 2. | A staff from the shop* |


\* The staff the candidate took or tried to use during the Test, if any. Otherwise, they choose one from the rack.

> *At my table:* The candidate took psychic damage from the illusionist and walked out with glassy eyes and the Pessimist madness. They tested Red.

### Failure

A candidate who goes down in the street still wakes in Wayreth, with an injury but no boon. If you want a harder fail state, make them a provisional member, as the module does, until the Conclave decides they've earned a full Test.
